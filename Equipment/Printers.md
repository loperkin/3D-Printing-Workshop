## Bambu Lab A1

My primary printer is the **Bambu Lab A1**.

I use it for functional parts, electronics enclosures, brackets, mounts, prototypes, and the occasional household repair.

Most of my projects involve making something useful rather than printing something just to put on a shelf.

### Printer Specifications

| Specification | Bambu Lab A1 |
|---|---|
| Build volume | 256 × 256 × 256 mm |
| Included nozzle | 0.4 mm stainless steel |
| Maximum nozzle temperature | 300°C |
| Maximum bed temperature | 100°C |
| Maximum toolhead speed | 500 mm/s |
| Supported materials | PLA, PETG, TPU, PVA |
| Slicer | Bambu Studio |

### What I Print

My printer gets used for projects such as:

- ESPHome Alarmo keypad enclosures
- MultiSensor cases and wall-mounted enclosures
- Custom brackets and adapters
- Electronics project boxes
- Replacement parts and household fixes
- Prototypes that usually need at least one revision

### Why I Use It

The A1 makes it practical to move from an idea to a physical part without spending most of the project troubleshooting the printer itself.

That doesn't mean every print comes out perfectly.

Sometimes the design needs another revision. Sometimes the settings need adjusting. And sometimes the first attempt teaches me exactly what not to do.

That's part of the process.
