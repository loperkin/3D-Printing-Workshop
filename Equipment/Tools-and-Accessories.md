## 🔧 Electronics Assembly Tools

A lot of my 3D printing projects involve electronics, so my workshop includes tools for assembling circuits, working with wiring, and fitting components into printed enclosures.

### Soldering Station

A soldering station is useful for assembling custom PCBs, attaching wires, and making permanent electrical connections.

I've used soldering equipment for projects including my ESPHome MultiSensor and Alarmo keypad.

**[Soldering Station — Compare Prices](https://geni.us/le9ZvwC)**

### Soldering Flux

Flux helps solder flow and form reliable connections.

It's especially useful when assembling small electronic components or working with custom PCBs.

**[Soldering Flux — Compare Prices](https://geni.us/ZWPR)**

### Wire Strippers

Good wire strippers make preparing connections easier and help avoid damaging conductors.

## 📏 Measuring & Fitting Parts

Designing functional prints often means measuring an existing object and creating a part that fits around it.

A few useful tools for this kind of work include:

### Digital Calipers

Useful for measuring:

- PCB dimensions
- Component spacing
- Screw holes
- Wall thickness
- Enclosure clearances
- Printed part tolerances

For functional printing, accurate measurements can save a surprising amount of wasted filament.

## 🧵 Filament Handling

Filament condition can have a noticeable impact on print quality, particularly with moisture-sensitive materials like PETG.

### Filament Storage

Keeping filament dry and protected from dust helps maintain consistent printing results.

### Filament Drying

When PETG absorbs moisture, print quality can suffer.

A filament dryer can help restore printing performance when used according to the filament manufacturer's recommendations.

## 🔬 What Makes a Tool Worth Keeping?

For me, a useful workshop tool should do at least one of three things:

1. Make a job easier.
2. Improve the quality or reliability of the finished project.
3. Help me solve a problem I couldn't easily solve otherwise.

I don't need the most expensive version of everything.

I need equipment that works.
